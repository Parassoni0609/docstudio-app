Core Privacy Architecture: Local-First
Your documents (PDFs, DOCX, TXT, images), research notes, canvases, annotations, and extracted text are stored strictly on your local device. We never upload or transmit your documents to cloud servers. On-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed entirely locally via offline Google ML Kit models.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information (Optional)

You can use DOCStudio as a Guest without providing personal details. When signing in with Google or Email:

  • Identifiers: Unique User ID (Firebase UID).
  • Profile Data: Display name, email address, and profile photo URL.
  • Metadata: Creation timestamp, last sign-in timestamp, and membership tier (free or pro).

B. Advertising Information (Free Tier Only)

To support free access, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • Diagnostic and ad interaction statistics.
  • Pro Tier subscribers experience an ad-free environment without ad requests.

C. Diagnostic & Technical Data

Aggregated technical diagnostics such as app crashes, device model, operating system version, and general locale may be processed via Firebase to improve stability.

2. Device Permissions

Permission Usage & Purpose
Camera Used only when you scan documents or capture images into a project. All images are processed on-device.
Storage / Media Used solely to import files and export research notes, packages, and reports selected by you.
Internet & Network State Required for Firebase account sign-in, subscription tier verification, and serving advertisements (Free tier).

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services whose privacy policies govern their data handling:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Document Data: Deleting projects, documents, or uninstalling the App purges all local document data from your device immediately.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete permanent deletion of your Firebase account and Firestore record at any time by emailing parasswarnkar0609@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

DOCStudio does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate removal.

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Users in the EEA, UK, California, and other jurisdictions have rights to access, rectify, delete, and port their personal data, as well as opt out of personalized ads via device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
